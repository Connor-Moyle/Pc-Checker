When hardware needs attention it raises an interrupt. The driver's interrupt service routine (ISR) runs immediately and should finish in microseconds. Anything slower is handed to a deferred procedure call (DPC). While an ISR or DPC runs, nothing else on that CPU core does, so a driver that spends too long here blocks games and audio. Healthy desktops stay under 1 % ISR time and 3 % DPC time; a single DPC should finish in under 1 ms.

Wi-Fi
Wi-Fi networks on the same channel take turns to talk. The more networks share yours, the slower and laggier it gets. The channel is set on the router, not on this PC. 'best' marks the least crowded of the non-overlapping 2.4 GHz channels (1, 6, 11) as seen from here.
Wi-Fi settings
Wi-Fi signal
Wi-Fi standard
Wi-Fi tuned
Wi-Fi uses three bands. 2.4 GHz reaches furthest but has only three channels that do not overlap (1, 6 and 11) and is shared with Bluetooth and microwaves. 5 GHz has many more channels and is much faster at shorter range. 6 GHz (Wi-Fi 6E and 7) is the fastest and emptiest, with the shortest range. Networks on the same channel take turns, so a crowded channel is slow even with a strong signal.

• Corners follow the monitor your mouse is on when the overlay is shown; use Custom to pin it to one spot.
• FPS counts the frames the game in front hands to Windows, the same way PresentMon does. It needs administrator rights, which PC Checker always runs with, and only runs while an FPS metric is shown.
• Frame time is how long each frame took (lower is smoother). 1% low is the frame rate of the slowest 1% of frames in the last 30 seconds: it shows stutter that the average hides.
• Readings come from the same sensors as the Temperatures page. RAM, network and disk activity are read from Windows directly, so they work even without hardware sensors.
• Some anti-cheat systems block overlays or hide a game's frames; the overlay then shows “--”. If a game refuses to start with the overlay on, turn it off for that game.
• Works over windowed and borderless-window games. Exclusive full-screen games hide every desktop window, including this one: switch the game to borderless.
