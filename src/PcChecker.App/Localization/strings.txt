Wi-Fi uses three bands. 2.4 GHz reaches furthest but has only three channels that do not overlap (1, 6 and 11) and is shared with Bluetooth and microwaves. 5 GHz has many more channels and is much faster at shorter range. 6 GHz (Wi-Fi 6E and 7) is the fastest and emptiest, with the shortest range. • Corners follow the monitor your mouse is on when the overlay is shown; use Custom to pin it to one spot.
• FPS counts the frames the game in front hands to Windows, the same way PresentMon does. It needs administrator rights, which PC Checker always runs with, and only runs while an FPS metric is shown.
• Frame time is how long each frame took (lower is smoother). 1% low is the frame rate of the slowest 1% of frames in the last 30 seconds: it shows stutter that the average hides.
• Readings come from the same sensors as the Temperatures page. RAM, network and disk activity are read from Windows directly, so they work even without hardware sensors.
• Some anti-cheat systems block overlays or hide a game's frames; the overlay then shows “--”. If a game refuses to start with the overlay on, turn it off for that game.
• Works over windowed and borderless-window games. Exclusive full-screen games hide every desktop window, including this one: switch the game to borderless.
